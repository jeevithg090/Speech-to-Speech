One model that hears and speaks

Your recording is turned into SNAC audio tokens, the audio-extended Sarvam-1 model answers with audio tokens, and SNAC turns them back into speech. A speaker embedding taken from your first turn keeps the reply voice consistent for the session.

  2. SNAC encode
  3. AudioLLM (Sarvam-1)
  4. SNAC decode
  5. Audio out

About this model

The repository ships the architecture and training code, not a trained speech checkpoint. Until you train one with scripts/train_audio_llm.py, the unified path returns arbitrary audio. That is expected.
